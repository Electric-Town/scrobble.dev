# Scrobble.dev expansion panel audit

Date: 11 August 2026

This review uses professional and user lenses. It does not claim that named public figures participated in or endorsed the work.

## Overall verdict

Approve a staged, knowledge-first expansion. Reject literal implementation of the research handoff's route counts, homepage modules, page symmetry and question-volume targets.

The existing editorial visual system is a useful foundation. The proposed scale would reintroduce machine-like repetition, cognitive overload and unsupported institutional authority.

## 50-lens result

- PASS: 15
- FLAG: 28
- BLOCK: 7

The blocking lenses were first-time comprehension, mobile navigation, ADHD, AuDHD, information architecture, full-inventory search and unbounded progressive disclosure.

## Red-team findings

1. Public output currently teaches the explicitly excluded adjacent identifier-resolution subject.
2. The catalogue calls 17 entries source-checked while the registry has no claim-level source identifiers, lifecycle or last-verified dates.
3. Governance follows profile publication in the handoff; this sequence must be reversed.
4. The proposed event envelope combines observation, mutable state, durable occurrence and transport concerns before a processing model exists.
5. Privacy metadata does not enforce privacy, and erasure can conflict with tombstones, receipts and backups.
6. The present OKF check validates a few frontmatter strings, not full conformance.
7. Machine-facing files duplicate volatile prose, dates and counts.
8. A generated 100-to-200-question benchmark would reward keyword routing before it could measure answer quality.

## Blue-team controls

1. Enforce the public boundary by scanning all generated outputs in CI.
2. Use claim-level provenance and explicit unknowns.
3. Publish governance, contribution terms and security reporting before normative work.
4. Define a processing model before schemas and conformance claims.
5. Separate common event metadata from observation, state, occurrence, mutation and capability payloads.
6. Generate factual human and machine views from the same records.
7. Validate the knowledge bundle against the complete OKF v0.2 conformance contract and pin the upstream specification revision.
8. Begin with 30 to 40 human-reviewed questions tied to shipped content.
9. Require an independent implementation pilot before candidate-profile language.

## Journalism panel

The narrator is Scrobble.dev as a maintained editorial reference. It attributes official requirements, project statements, observations, inferences and proposals differently. Current claims include a date or version. Unknown remains visible.

The panel rejected invisible-expert narration, detached source lists, self-certified neutrality, decorative slogans, global freshness labels and issue reports presented as proof of current defects.

## Frontier panel

Machine discoverability must amplify verified knowledge rather than multiply claims. `llms.txt` remains an experimental index. JSON-LD must match visible content. Derived assets must carry status, version, provenance and canonical URLs without becoming an independent editorial corpus.

Automated retrieval evaluation starts after useful routes exist and measures evidence fidelity, freshness, ambiguity, appropriate abstention and public-scope leakage.

## ADHD and AuDHD panel

Seven top-level destinations, ten homepage modules and many equal cards create too many competing decisions. The approved contract limits primary navigation to five destinations, reduces the homepage to five editorial bands, places the answer before page contents and keeps optional depth contextual.

Literal labels, stable page patterns and predictable back paths take precedence over clever interaction. Essential definitions and safety information may not be collapsed.

## Nielsen review

- Visibility: announce result counts, sorting and freshness.
- Real-world match: start with user questions and define protocol terms later.
- Control: URL-backed state, Reset all and removable filters.
- Consistency: stable Learn and project patterns without cloned prose.
- Prevention: validate evidence and distinguish unknown from false.
- Recognition: breadcrumbs, section indexes and visible active filters.
- Flexibility: search, anchors, keyboard access and downloads.
- Minimalism: five primary destinations and five homepage bands.
- Recovery: useful no-result and stale-source states.
- Help: contextual contribution paths.

## Gestalt review

Group information by task rather than technical taxonomy. Use proximity and common region for filters, results and source metadata. Preserve one visual reading path. Avoid turning every fact into a card. Use disclosure to contain optional detail, never to conceal the page's answer.

## AI-slop review

Remove:

- symmetrical slogans and poetic fragment headings;
- repeated card grids and alternating colour bands;
- cloned media pages;
- repeated `X is not Y` constructions;
- generic `Why it matters`, `Key takeaways` and `Deep dive` sections;
- internal terms such as funnel, surface and content architecture in public copy;
- self-certifying adjectives and artificial quantity targets.

Retain:

- restrained colour and typography;
- flat editorial rules;
- real tables;
- visible sources and status;
- short field definitions;
- strong keyboard focus;
- no gradients, glow, blur or continuous motion.

## Approved release contract

The detailed implementation contract is recorded in [the knowledge-first expansion design](../plans/2026-08-11-knowledge-first-expansion-design.md).

Floppy remains in the global footer with FloppyDesktop. The footer also retains the Scrobble.dev repository and both sponsor links. Relevant pages include a small contribution path to the guide and to Floppy, but neither interrupts the page's primary task or defines the publication through one implementation. The knowledge bundle must conform to OKF v0.2 and link to its official specification.
